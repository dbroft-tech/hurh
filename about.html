Who We Are

Grace Community Church began in 2005 with a simple vision: to bring the hope of the Gospel to the people of Springfield. Since then, we've grown into a community committed to worship, discipleship, and service — but our heart hasn't changed.

We are a non-denominational church that believes in the power of Scripture, the love of God, and the importance of authentic community. Our doors are open to everyone, and our mission is clear: love God, love people, and serve the world.

Whether you're taking your first steps in faith or have been walking with God for decades, you'll find a warm welcome and a place to grow here.

What We Believe

  • We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God.
  • We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, who died for our sins and rose again.
  • We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
  • We believe in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit, who empowers believers to live godly lives.
  • We believe in the Church as the body of Christ, called to worship, discipleship, and mission.
